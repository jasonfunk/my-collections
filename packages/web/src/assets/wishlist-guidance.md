# Jason's Toy Wishlist

These are vintage toys from the late '70s and '80s that I've been hunting
for. Here's what to look for so you can spot a good one!

The main thing is that they're originals — the real old ones, not modern
re-releases or reproductions. If anything says "reissue" or "commemorative"
on it, that's not the one.

They should come with all their little accessories and weapons (the list
below notes what each one needs). A little wear from age is totally fine —
these are 40+ year old toys — but they should look nice on a shelf, stand
up on their own, and not be broken.

Loose (out of the box) is actually what I prefer, so don't spend extra for
original packaging. Stickers applied are great; unapplied or a little faded
is fine too. Some yellowing on the plastic is normal. The one thing I'd
really like to avoid is any smoke or mildew smell — worth asking the seller
before buying.

For Transformers specifically: I display them in robot mode, so the big
thing is that they can stand up and ideally still transform. If the seller
mentions transformation issues, that's usually a pass.
