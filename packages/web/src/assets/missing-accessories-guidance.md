# Missing Accessories

This is a parts-hunting list — items in the collection where at least one
accessory is unaccounted for. Bring it to shows, flea markets, or use it
when browsing eBay to track down replacements or reproductions.

A few things to keep in mind:

- The accessory lists come from catalog data, which may not be 100% complete
  for every variant. When in doubt, cross-reference a guide like the
  Tomart's Action Figure Digest or the Transformerland wiki.

- Reproduction accessories are widely available and often indistinguishable
  from originals — great for display purposes, less so for strict completeness.

- Condition matters less for accessories than for figures. A slightly worn
  blaster is fine as long as it's the right one.
