# Missing Accessories

This is a parts-hunting list — items in the collection where at least one accessory is unaccounted for.

A few things to keep in mind:

- The accessory lists come from catalog data, which may not be 100% complete for every variant. When in doubt, cross-reference a guide like the Tomart's Action Figure Digest or the Transformerland wiki.
- Reproduction accessories are widely available and often indistinguishable from originals — great for display purposes, less so for strict completeness.
- Condition matters less for accessories than for figures. A slightly worn blaster is fine as long as it's the right one.
